# CSE200 Technical Writing and Presentation

**Credits:** 0.75 | **Hours per week:** 3 (in alternate weeks)

## Topics Covered

- Issues of technical writing and effective oral presentation in Computer Science and Engineering
- Writing styles of definitions, propositions, theorems, and proofs
- Preparation of reports, research papers, theses:
  - Abstract, preface, contents, bibliography, and index
- Writing of book reviews and referee reports
- Writing tools: LaTeX
- Diagram drawing software
- Presentation tools and techniques
